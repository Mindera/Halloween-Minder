How to play

  • Move with the D-pad, a swipe on the maze, or the arrow keys / WASD.
  • Shoot eyeballs with FIRE, a tap on the maze, or Space.
  • Grab every IT bit (0s, 1s, floppies, chips, bugs…) and bonk every monster to clear the level.
  • You have 2 minutes per level. If a monster touches you, it's game over!
  • Levels get faster and sneakier… all the way to level 100. Level 12 is a surprise party! 🎉
